UPGRADE 02 · MID-WORKOUT

Active rest-timer countdown ring

References run a countdown ring between sets (0:28, Pause). The app already knows when a set is logged — that's the natural trigger.

ImpactHIGH
Current

Log a set, then guess your rest. No timer at all — you either glance at a phone clock or wing it. The 0/N ring tracks set progress, not rest.

Proposed

Auto rest ring after each set

08 · SCOPE DISCIPLINE

What we deliberately skip.

Half of what makes those Dribbble shots look rich is content-platform machinery. It's beautiful — and wrong for us. Gym Trainer is a personal tracker, not a media app.

✕

Video programs

A whole content library and player to build and maintain. We track your training — we don't produce workouts to watch.

✕

Trainer chat

Live coaching means humans, moderation and support load. Out of scope for a self-directed logger.

✕

Community feed

Social feeds bring accounts, moderation and comparison anxiety. This app is between you and your training.

✕

Stock athlete photography

Hero photos of models make a marketing site, not a tool. Our own data — the muscle map, the streak — is the imagery.
