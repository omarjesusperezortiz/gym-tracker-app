How to read it: "Pull" (program) lists movements — Vertical pull, Horizontal row, Biceps curl. Each movement is filled by one specific exercise (the "Chosen" one, which carries the gif + steps + logs). You can swap it for another exercise that trains the same movement/muscle — that's the flexibility you keep.

What changes in the data

Bottom line: your instinct is exactly right. Program = Pull. Movement = "Vertical pull" (the muscle-group pattern — what you train). Exercise = "Cable Lat Pulldown" (the specific thing you actually do, with its gif + steps + history, referenced by a stable id). Today we only have levels 1 & 2 as loose text; the change adds level 3 as a real, swappable, code-controlled entity.
